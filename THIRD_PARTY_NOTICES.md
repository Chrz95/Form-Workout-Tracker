# Third-party content

- **Free Exercise DB**: 876 exercise records and 1,746 original photographic positions from
  https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db, declared public domain under the Unlicense by its maintainer.
  Frames are converted to WebP and resized for this application. Dataset instructions are retained as source content.
- **Unsplash**: studio hero image served at
  https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1534438327276-14e5300c3a48.
  Used under the Unsplash license; no exercise-technique claim is attached to the decorative hero.
- **DM Sans** and **Manrope**: local font files from Google Fonts; SIL Open Font License terms in `licenses/`.
- **Lucide**: interface icons supplied by the `lucide-react` dependency, under its ISC license.

Dependency licensing remains in each installed package. No private source files or user training records are bundled.
