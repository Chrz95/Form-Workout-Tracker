Exercise catalog sources

Imported exercise text and media retain their own licenses. Every imported record identifies its source in the exercise details. Exercise descriptions are source-provided; the application does not represent them as individually reviewed coaching.

  • Free Exercise DB: the original 876 records and photographic frames. Unlicense / public domain notice.
  • Hasan Yıldırım Exercise Dataset: English instructions and metadata only. Copyright (c) 2026 Hasan Emir Yıldırım. MIT license and media exception. No Gym visual images or GIFs are copied.
  • Glowupp Open ExerciseDB: descriptions, execution tips and metadata. Copyright (c) 2026 Glowupp-app. MIT license.
  • Kinetic Place Exercise Database: instructions and metadata. Copyright (c) 2026 Kinetic.place. MIT license.
  • Arham Wani Exercise Metadata: generated and curated source descriptions and steps; no purchased videos or thumbnails. Copyright (c) 2026 Arham Wani. MIT license.
  • wger Community Exercise Catalog: English descriptions, attributed images and original video links. Each description uses its English translation's license and creator credits. Each image/video carries independent author and license information in the details. Applicable licenses: CC-BY-SA 3.0, CC-BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 1.0.

Changes and attribution

Names, muscle/equipment labels and text formatting are normalized for discovery; wger HTML is rendered as plain text. New duplicate names are suppressed while existing identities are retained. Descriptions are not invented to inflate the catalog. wger text adaptations retain their original ShareAlike licenses. Images are proportionally resized and converted to WebP; real animation frames are retained and static first-frame posters allow stopped motion. Original author credits and source links appear with each imported exercise and asset.

Unattributed non-public-domain wger descriptions, unattributed images, one AI-marked image, and media without permission to redistribute were excluded. Selected videos have local H264 copies and thumbnails; other licensed links stream from their original host.

Custom exercises

Custom exercises belong to the private training API and are not part of the public static catalog. A custom image URL remains a link to its original host; users should supply content they are permitted to use.
